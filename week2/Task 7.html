How to Make a Simple Sandwich

What You Need

  • 2 slices of bread
  • 2 slices of cheese
  • Tomato
  • Lettuce
  • Butter or mayo

Steps

  1. Place the bread on a clean table.
  2. Spread butter or mayo on one side of each slice.
  3. Add lettuce and tomato on one slice.
  4. Put the cheese on top and close the sandwich.
  5. Be careful not to press too hard or the bread may tear.
  6. Cut in half and serve fresh.
