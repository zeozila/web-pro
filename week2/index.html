Week 2 — HTML Practice Exercises

These are simple beginner-to-intermediate HTML tasks completed in a student-friendly style.

Exercise 1 — HTML Skeleton

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <meta name="viewport" content="width=device-width, initial-scale=1.0">
    <title>My First Page</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Hello, Gachon!</h1>
  </body>
</html>

Exercise 2 — Headings, Paragraphs and Emphasis

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <title>My Hobby</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>Reading</h1>

    <h2>Why I Love It</h2>
    <p>I love reading because it helps me relax and makes me feel calm. It also gives me a lot of <strong>focus</strong> and imagination.</p>

    <h2>How to Start</h2>
    <p>You can begin with a short story or a novel that interests you. Reading for just a few minutes every day can build a good <em>reading habit</em>.</p>
  </body>
</html>

Exercise 7 — Ordered Steps with Nested List

<!DOCTYPE html>
<html lang="en">
  <head>
    <meta charset="UTF-8">
    <title>My Recipe</title>
  </head>
  <body>
    <h1>How to Make a Simple Sandwich</h1>

    <h2>What You Need</h2>
    <ul>
      <li>2 slices of bread</li>
      <li>2 slices of cheese</li>
      <li>Tomato</li>
      <li>Lettuce</li>
      <li>Butter or mayo</li>
    </ul>

    <h2>Steps</h2>
    <ol>
      <li>Place the bread on a clean table.</li>
      <li>Spread butter or mayo on one side of each slice.</li>
      <li>Add lettuce and tomato on one slice.</li>
      <li>Put the cheese on top and close the sandwich.</li>
      <li><strong>Be careful</strong> not to press too hard or the bread may tear.</li>
      <li>Cut in half and serve <em>fresh</em>.</li>
    </ol>
  </body>
</html>
